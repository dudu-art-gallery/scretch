DUDU

I feared God and God only. I was at the edge of my lifespan. It was rough.

I forgave my closest and looked one last time into their faces and gave them a kiss. The last thing I could do. God forgave me, but I could never forgive my past self.

I prayed my last prayer and was ready to die. Not because I wanted to die, but because I was ready to die. September, 2024: lying in bed at the hospital. I am lost in the voices I am hearing.

I am sleeping alone, but not entirely alone, because I have God on my side.

- Me, "DUDU" – I dedicate all my paintings to the psychologically disabled people in the world. This collection is a summary between order and chaos: life, from being unable to fly to the liberating moment of breaking out from the cocoon and just flying.
